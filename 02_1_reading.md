### Week 2 reading

We'll use this *Tribolium* (flour beetle) model system as an example to build out the fundamental concepts of process models, including population dynamics, community dynamics, and rapid evolutionary processes. It might seem complex on first read but in a few weeks you'll be able to make models like these!

Melbourne BA, Hastings A (2008). Extinction risk depends strongly on factors contributing to stochasticity. **Nature** 454: 100-103. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06922.

Be sure to read the [supplement](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fnature06922/MediaObjects/41586_2008_BFnature06922_MOESM4_ESM.pdf). This is where the models are described from this paper. These are  models of individual-level processes, like births and deaths, that drive population-level dynamics, like fluctuations, population cycles, and extinction.
